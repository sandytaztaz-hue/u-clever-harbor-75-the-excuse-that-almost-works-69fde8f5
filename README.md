Entry for the [The Excuse That Almost Works](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work during GTA 6 launch week —
15 words, 82 characters, plain text, no setup, no signature:

> Sorry, jury duty called me in, court starts 9:30 and phones are banned till lunch.

Why this line: jury duty is a real, boring, specific commitment a tired manager
has heard before and can almost believe — and "phones are banned till lunch"
is the excuse's own logic doing the work: the text itself is sent in a hurry
before the phones go away. The field is full of plumbers, boilers, dentists
and root canals; nobody is called for jury duty.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same
single line on every run, no randomness, no network, no clock.
